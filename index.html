Heicly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Heicly is a photo viewer and converter for Windows. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: your photos never leave your PC, and we do not collect any personal data.

Your photos and files

Heicly opens, shows and converts the files you choose entirely on your own computer. Photos, their metadata (such as date, camera and location) and the converted files are never uploaded, shared or sent to us or to anyone else. Converted files are saved only where you choose.

No account, no tracking

Heicly has no sign-in and no account. It contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.

Network use

Heicly works offline. The only network connection it makes is to the Microsoft Store, through Windows, to check whether you own Heicly Pro and to let you buy it. Purchases are processed by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; we receive no payment details. Microsoft may give developers aggregated, anonymous Store statistics (such as install counts and crash reports), as described in Microsoft's terms.

Data stored on your PC

Heicly keeps its settings (for example your chosen output folder and quality) and, if something goes wrong, a local error log in your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Heicly). These files stay on your PC and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Heicly removes them.

Children

Heicly does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
